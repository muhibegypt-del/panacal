Spectral corrections (.ccss) for the Spyder5 / i1Display. AutoCal picks the
one that best matches the connected TV's model (55G2 over any G2, over the
same model year, over a generic WOLED one), and never an LCD one. It also
looks in the ArgyllCMS and DisplayCAL folders.
